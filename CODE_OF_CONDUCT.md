# Code of Conduct

Kre8ivTech public repositories are a professional space. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other interaction in those repositories.

## Expected

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Ask when something is unclear.
- Keep client information, credentials, and other private data out of issues, pull requests, and commits.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and sharing private or confidential information.

## Reporting

Email [info@kre8ivtech.com](mailto:info@kre8ivtech.com). The studio reviews reports and may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, or block an account.
