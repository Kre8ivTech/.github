## Who we are

We're **Kre8ivTech**, an independent, founder-led software studio in **San Antonio, Texas**. We help small businesses, nonprofits, and growing organizations turn ideas and everyday operational challenges into useful software.

Founded by **Jeremiah Castillo**, Kre8ivTech is veteran-owned and minority-owned. Our approach brings creative thinking and engineering discipline together: understand the work, design the right system, and stay involved as it grows.

Our mission is simple: **make powerful technology approachable, so organizations can run on software built for how they actually work.**

## How we work

**Design → Develop → Deploy → Evolve**

We start with the people, workflows, and outcomes behind a project. Then we build, test, document, and deliver it—with a plan for what comes after launch.

- **Clarity:** Plain language, understandable decisions, and software you can own.
- **Accountability:** A founder-led relationship with a person responsible for the work.
- **Continuity:** Maintenance and improvements that keep pace with your organization.
- **Thoughtful AI:** AI assists our research, development, and testing under human direction and review. [Read our AI usage policy.](https://www.kre8ivtech.com/ai-usage-policy)

The **8** in our name becomes a continuous loop: creativity and engineering, moving from the first sketch to the next improvement.
