**Kre8ivTech, LLC** is a veteran-owned AI-forward software studio founded September 3, 2009. We are a San Antonio, TX service-area business serving organizations across the United States.

Founder & Visionary **Jeremiah Castillo** is a retired U.S. Air Force veteran with 24 years of service.

## What we build

- **AI-integrated custom software**
- **AI automation and agents**, with a person kept in the loop
- **Web and mobile apps**
- **Practical help** for organizations adopting AI

## Products

| Product | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **KT-Portal** | Multi-tenant client portal with ticketing, invoicing, contracts, a knowledge base, and role-based access. |
| **Kre8ivLabs AI** | Automated website generation. |
| **OpsAgenda** | A daily brief built from your connected productivity tools that prioritizes tasks and shows your current capacity. |

## How we work

AI agents do the repetitive work. People approve:

- Scope
- Merges
- Anything client-facing
